# Security Policy

Trayvi is not production- or pilot-ready. Do not place real customer, identity, location, payment,
or credential data in this repository or its development environments.

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to the repository owner. Do not open a public issue with
secrets or exploit details. Rotate any exposed credential immediately, preserve minimal evidence,
and follow the incident owner designated by the deployment environment.

The baseline policy is defined in [SECURITY_PRINCIPLES.md](SECURITY_PRINCIPLES.md). Production
reporting contacts, SLAs, and disclosure terms remain a release blocker until repository hosting and
operational ownership are selected.
